Case studies

01

Purchase contract review agent

The problem

Upstate New York residential contracts are often signed subject to attorney approval, which leaves a short window to review the contract and approve, disapprove, or propose changes. Commercial purchase and sale agreements carry more terms and more dates to track.

What it does

  • Extracts deal terms into a deal abstract, with a section citation for every term
  • Calculates critical dates under New York General Construction Law, including weekend and holiday rollovers
  • Flags risks against separate residential and commercial playbooks
  • Drafts the approval letter or comments and the client email for attorney review

Scope

Residential 1 to 4 family broker contracts and commercial purchase and sale agreements.

Design principle

The agent drafts. The attorney decides. Nothing leaves the office without attorney review.

Built with

Claude, as a custom Skill.

02

Implementation workflow-mapping agent

The problem

Configuring a prosecutor or civil case management system for a government office meant mapping the work of every position in that office, then synthesizing those workflows into a single system configuration.

What it did

Structured the workflow analysis behind each implementation. Over the final two years, Claude and Microsoft Copilot were built into daily delivery work, speeding workflow analysis, system configuration, and preparation of training materials.

Note

Built for internal use at a prior employer and described here in general terms.
