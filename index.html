Case studies

Case study 1

Purchase contract review agent

The problem

Upstate New York residential contracts are often signed subject to attorney approval, which leaves a short window to review the contract and approve, disapprove, or propose changes. Commercial purchase and sale agreements carry more terms and more dates to track.

Scope
Residential 1 to 4 family broker contracts and commercial purchase and sale agreements.
Built with
Claude, as a custom Skill.

What it does

  1. 1

    Extracts deal terms into a deal abstract, with a section citation for every term

  2. 2

    Calculates critical dates under New York General Construction Law, including weekend and holiday rollovers

  3. 3

    Flags risks against separate residential and commercial playbooks

  4. 4

    Drafts the approval letter or comments and the client email for attorney review

Case study 2

Commercial contract review agent

The problem

Commercial agreements from the other side often bury non-standard terms in liability, indemnification, data and renewal clauses. Reviewing them clause by clause against your standard positions is slow, and a missed auto-renewal or uncapped indemnity costs the client money.

Scope
SaaS, services, vendor, licensing agreements and NDAs. It works from either the customer side or the vendor side, and you pick the side at the start of each review.
Built with
Claude, as a custom Skill.

What it does

  1. 1

    Pulls out the key commercial terms (parties, term and renewal, fees, liability cap, indemnities, IP, data protection, termination), with a section citation for each

  2. 2

    Checks each clause against a playbook of standard and fallback positions, and marks it acceptable, negotiable or must-change

  3. 3

    Drafts a redline with suggested replacement language for every flagged clause

  4. 4

    Produces an issues list ranked by business impact, plus a client email summarizing the main points, all for attorney review
